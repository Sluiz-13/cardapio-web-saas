# Menuair — brag plan

**What:** Menuair is a multi-tenant digital menu and restaurant management SaaS for Brazilian restaurants, covering orders, tables, the cash register, delivery and Pix. It charges no commission per order.
**For whom:** Brazilian restaurant owners who are tired of losing a cut of every sale to marketplace apps.
**Differentiator:** It is the restaurant's own sales channel. Pix goes straight into the restaurant's account, it charges 0% commission, and it covers everything "from the first order to closing the register".
**Hook:** A giant "0%" slams in, followed by "de comissão sobre suas vendas."
**Visual identity:** Brand orange #D35400, dark slate #111827 and warm cream #FCFBF8. Inter ExtraBold for headlines, Fraunces for the cardápio accent. The real screenshots from `public/screenshots/` are used throughout.
**Tone:** default, which is punchy, clean and confident. Copy is in PT-BR and taken from the landing page.
**Share caption:** Pedidos, mesas, caixa e delivery num lugar só, and 0% de comissão.
