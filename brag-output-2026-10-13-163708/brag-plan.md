# Menuair: long version (feature tour) brag plan

**Angle:** it opens on the same "0%" hook as the short cut, then gives a guided tour. One PC monitor stays on screen while 8 real admin screens rotate through it, each with a numbered chip and a two-line caption.
**Source material:** real `/admin/*` pages captured from the local dev server, logged in as a **demo tenant** ("Menuair Burger", slug `menuair-burger-demo`, created by `work/seed-demo.ts` in the local DB only). The customer phone screens are the real screenshots from `public/screenshots/`.
**Tone:** default, which is punchy and clean. Copy is PT-BR, taken from the landing page and the panel's own subtitles where possible.
